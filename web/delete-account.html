Delete Account & Data - AES Portal

Last updated: 4 August 2026

AES Portal is an internal operations app built and operated by Al Areesh Engineering Solutions ("AES", "we", "us") for our own employees, vendors, and contractors. Accounts are created by an AES administrator - there is no public sign-up - so account deletion is handled directly by an administrator on your behalf.

How to Request Deletion

To request that your AES Portal account and associated data be deleted, do either of the following:

  • Ask your AES administrator (Back Office or CEO) to deactivate and delete your account from the Manage Employees screen in the app, or
  • Email mukhtarfarhan22@gmail.com with your username and the request. We will action it within 14 days and confirm by email.

What Gets Deleted

  • Deleted: your login credentials, account profile (username, role, region), and your ability to sign in to the app.
  • Retained: business records you created or were associated with while using the app - such as work orders, quotations, invoices, expenses, vendor bills, and attendance history - are kept as part of AES's operational and financial record-keeping, even after your account is deleted. These records are not linked to a working login once your account is removed.

Retention Period

Account credentials and profile data are deleted within 14 days of a verified request. Business records described above are retained for as long as required for AES's operational and financial record-keeping obligations.

Contact

Questions about this process can be sent to mukhtarfarhan22@gmail.com.
